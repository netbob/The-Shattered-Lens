# Character Manifest: The Shattered Lens

This file establishes the immutable lineages, organizational affiliations, and narrative fates for the primary and secondary character arcs across the multi-volume tracking schema of `netbob/The-Shattered-Lens`.

---

## I. The Thorne Lineage

### 1. Marcus Thorne
*   **Role:** The Scribe / Founder of The Registry.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Infrastructure systems operator and amateur packet-radio coordinator (Clovis, CA).
*   **Current Location:** Pod 1 Command Map Room, Pine Ridge SAC Facility.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Older brother to Dr. Aris Thorne.
*   **Core Assets:** Book I of the Central Registry (Leather ledger), Universal Geneve mechanical artillery stopwatch `[ITEM-009]`, custom leaf-spring cold chisel `[ITEM-012]`.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Survives Act I/II as the central administrative and judicial authority at Pine Ridge. Establishes the handwritten copperplate credit economy to prevent local societal regression into cannibalism.

### 2. Dr. Aris Thorne
*   **Role:** The Observer / Founder of the Haleakala Mountain Node.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Senior Astrophysicist, Pan-STARRS 2 Telescope Array (Haleakala, HI).
*   **Current Location:** Site 1 Summit Tracking Gallery, Hawaii.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Younger brother to Marcus Thorne.
*   **Core Assets:** 1.8-meter primary aperture telescope, visual refractor eyepiece retrofitted to Cassegrain cage, oilskin grid logbook, 1912 lithographed *Astrophysical Journal* spectrum chart.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Survives the Point Conception impact due to high-altitude positioning (10,030 feet). Re-allocates the dead 1.47-gigapixel GPC2 camera deck to pure analog visual transit and spectral tracking. Establishes independent data link to the California ridge via shortwave ionospheric mirror-bounce.

## II. The Vance Lineage

### 1. Elena Vance
*   **Role:** The Keeper of the Grid / Chief Infrastructure Engineer.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** High-voltage regional lineman and electrical substation automation technician (San Joaquin Valley, CA).
*   **Current Location:** Equipment decks / Pod 1, Pine Ridge.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Daughter to The Director (Vance); niece to Janice Vance.
*   **Core Assets:** Winchester 30-30 rifle, 5-foot carbon-steel railroad pry-bar `[ITEM-001]`, analog wire-gauge wheel, drop-forged 1/2-inch box wrench `[ITEM-014]`.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Broke from her father's centralized corporate digital network model before the collapse to focus on physical grid mechanics with Marcus. Survives to mechanically stabilize the deep well pump matrices (`Module 5E`) and serves as the structural enforcement pillar of the Registry.

### 2. The Director (Vance)
*   **Role:** The Priest of the Logic Void / Primary Antagonist.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Regional Director for the Pacific Coast Telecommunications Utility.
*   **Current Location:** Hardened Switching Vault, Shaver Lake, CA.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Father to Elena Vance; brother to Janice Vance.
*   **Core Assets:** Hardened IBM AS/400 mainframe enclosed in a legacy military-grade Faraday cage, backup digital land titles, enterprise database records.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Rules **The Synod** using an ideology of digital restoration. Attempts to force survivors into subjection by using rotting magnetic sector records as leverage for pre-impact property ownership. Executed/hanged by his own panicked technicians in Act IV after absolute bit-rot completely corrupts the mainframe memory layers.

### 3. Janice Vance
*   **Role:** Lead Infrastructure Engineer (NSA-South Power Grid).
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Power-switching controller, NSA-South Cryptographic Infrastructure Complex.
*   **Current Location:** Paradox Basin Vaults, Moab, Utah (Deceased).
*   **Lineage Connection:** Sister to The Director; estranged aunt to Elena Vance.
*   **Narrative Fate:** **Perishes during Epoch Zero** (`Module 1C`). Trapped inside the high-voltage subterranean breaker deck during the recursive logic rollover loop; killed instantly when the 6,000-amp switchgear detonates due to an unauthenticated timing mismatch with the Boulder atomic clock array.

## III. The Miller Lineage

### 1. Arthur Miller
*   **Role:** Registered Refugee `[INDEX 0412]`.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Retired diesel mechanic, Clovis municipal irrigation pool.
*   **Current Location:** Dead-storage chamber, Surface Shaft 3, Pine Ridge (Deceased).
*   **Lineage Connection:** Husband to Martha Miller; father to June Miller.
*   **Narrative Fate:** **First internal casualty of the Registry** (`Module 4E`). Successfully registers at the Foothill Ridge Gate on October 26 but succumbs to acute sub-micron silica inhalation on November 2. Name rule-slashed from active credit lines; body sealed in oilcloth wrap within the unheated frost-vaults.

### 2. Martha Miller
*   **Role:** Registered Refugee / Secondary Shaft Resident.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Homemaker and civilian tracker (Clovis, CA).
*   **Current Location:** Shaft 2 living quarters, Pine Ridge facility.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Wife to Arthur Miller (Deceased); mother to June Miller.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Survives the initial winter transition inside the subterranean shelter blocks. Defends her husband's physical identity against immediate ledger erasure during the `Module 4E` balance settlement.

### 3. June Miller
*   **Role:** Junior Observer / Astrometric Analyst.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Graduate Research Assistant, University of Hawaii Department of Astronomy.
*   **Current Location:** Site 1 Summit Tracking Gallery, Haleakala.
*   **Lineage Connection:** Daughter to Arthur and Martha Miller.
*   **Core Assets:** Brass surveyor's transit, mechanical weight-drop governor clockwork assembly.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Co-founds the Hawaii Mountain Node alongside Aris Thorne (`Module 2C`, `3C`). Manually maintains the sidereal tracking weights of the 1.8-meter telescope using raw mechanical physics. **Remains completely cut off from the mainland; has no knowledge that her father died on November 2 in California.**

## IV. Unaffiliated Core Registrants

### 1. David Sterling
*   **Role:** The Disconnected Router / Technical Vagabond.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Systems Administrator, NSA Cryptographic Infrastructure Complex (Pod 4).
*   **Current Location:** High sandstone rifts, Book Cliffs, Utah (In Transit).
*   **Core Assets:** Laser-milled un-magnetized tool-steel plate (Hex-root directory verification template), mechanical balance-wheel pocket watch.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Bypassed the pneumatic safety blockades of the Moab dome during the initial facility power short (`Module 2D`). Migrates east into the desert plateau on a long-arc survival trek before looping back west toward Shaver Lake in later acts, where he becomes a critical resource for the Synod's failing hardware engineers.

### 2. Robert Cross
*   **Role:** Labor Group Coordinator / Registered Refugee.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Irrigation district infrastructure supervisor (San Joaquin Valley).
*   **Current Location:** Shaft 3 bunk assignments / Manual well pump crews, Pine Ridge.
*   **Core Assets:** Rusted iron mechanical wrench, rope-wrapped winter boots.
*   **Narrative Fate:** Registered at the gate line on October 26 under `Module 3D` after surrendering his dead handset. Becomes the functional voice for the 532 refugees living in the dark lower shafts, leading the labor groups who operate the deep manual well-cranks.

### 3. Robert Hayes
*   **Role:** Conscripted Synod Infiltrator.
*   **Pre-Impact Status:** Dependent youth inside the Shaver Lake enclosure.
*   **Current Location:** Shaver Lake Switching Center (Act I/II) → Registry Captive (Act IV).
*   **Narrative Fate:** Sent up the mountain by the Director in Chapter 10 to execute a midnight black-ops raid to seize Marcus's paper books to use as a database calibration source. Captured in the map room by Marcus and Elena, spared due to his status as a desperate conscript fighting to protect his mother's digital index, and sent back to Shaver Lake with an open asylum terms message in Chapter 11.
